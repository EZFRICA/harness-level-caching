**Reading.**

- naive stopped at turn 198 of 200: its history reached 16385 tokens, one over the model's 16384 context. Up to then 99.6% of its prompt tokens were served from the prefix cache.
- cama with decision 0013 ran all 200 turns with 95.8% of its prompt served from cache and its last request at 3706 tokens as counted by the server (the table's last request column is CAMA's own estimate); plain cama was 34.3% cached. On the recall scenario 0013 took cama from 62.5% to 92.3%.
- Recall with this 7B model was 9 and 10 of 13 for the cama arms against 13 for naive; with DeepSeek-V4-Flash on Nebius the same arms recalled 13 (docs/measured/recall.md): the extracting model limits recall here.
- cama with 0013 had the slowest median turn over 200 turns (4.4 s against 3.0 s plain cama); its extraction calls share the same GPU as the answers, a cause not isolated here.

**Limits.**

- One run per row, one GPU, one 7B model; the client ran on a laptop through an SSH tunnel to a US region, so turn times include that network path.
- Per-token prices are 0 (self-hosted); the cost is GPU time, not reported per run.
- The cama with 0013 run over 200 turns was repeated after an accidental VM reset cut the first attempt at turn 139 (kept apart, not frozen).
