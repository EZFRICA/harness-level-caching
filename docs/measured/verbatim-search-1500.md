**Reading.**

- The message that last stated the probed value was recalled at 20 of 24 probes with MiniLM storing every message, and at 23 of 24 with EmbeddingGemma 2 or with statements only.
- The one probe missed by every arm (turn 147) came before the first fold, so its statement was still in the unfolded history, which the search skips.
- Every arm added quotes on 1273 to 1278 of 1500 turns; statements only cut the recalled messages from about 2530 to about 2410, and quotes sent once cut the estimated prompt tokens by about 2.5 percent.

**Limits.**

- Echo model: no answer, so no fact recall, no provider cache and no cost are measured; the echo model's short answers fold the summary later than a real model.
- One run per arm; one generated English scenario; EmbeddingGemma 2 thresholds carried over from MiniLM by percentile on this scenario (in sample).
