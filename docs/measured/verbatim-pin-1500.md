**Reading.**

- The message that last stated the probed value was in the prompt word for word at 24 of 24 probes with pinning, with or without HC_PIN_SUMMARY=rest, and with the H9 search under either embedder; compact alone had it at 4 of 24, only where it was not folded yet.
- Pinning added no quote on any turn and kept 56 distinct statements at the end; H9 added quotes on 1273 of 1500 turns.
- The pinned arms sent about 20 percent more prompt tokens than compact alone and about 12 percent more than H9.
- Memory time per turn had a median of 1.9 to 2.3 ms for the compact arms against 3.9 to 4.1 ms for H9, whose p95 was 57 ms with MiniLM and 170 ms with EmbeddingGemma 2 in these runs.

**Limits.**

- Echo model: no answer, so no fact recall, no provider cache and no cost are measured; the echo summary does not shrink, so HC_PIN_SUMMARY=rest cannot show a smaller prompt here (its fold input fell from 53684 to 49458 tokens).
- One run per arm, all in the container; one generated English scenario whose 341 statements reduce to 56 distinct texts; latency tails vary between runs (H9 p95 was 10 and 64 ms in the runs of figure verbatim-search-1500).
