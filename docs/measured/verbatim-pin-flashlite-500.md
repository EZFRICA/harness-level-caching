**Reading.**

- Compact alone recalled 23 of 24 facts: the sister's birthday, stated at turn 37 and folded before its probe at turn 148, was not in the summary the model read; both pinned arms recalled 24 of 24 with the statement in the prompt word for word at every probe.
- The pinned arms cost 0.478 and 0.479 USD against 0.455 for compact alone, about 5 percent more, for about 13 percent more prompt tokens; the provider served 36 percent of their prompt from cache against 33 percent.
- Leaving pinned statements out of the fold did not shrink the summary: its median was 545 estimated tokens against 453 with them, over 4 folds each.

**Limits.**

- One run per arm; one generated English scenario of 500 turns; the compact arm ran on one Gemini API key and the pinned arms on another, on different days, so the cache share and the turn times are not attributable to the arm alone.
- Recall is scored by string match on the answer; one missed fact in one run is an observation, not a rate.
