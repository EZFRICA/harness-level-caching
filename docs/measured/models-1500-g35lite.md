**Reading.**

- compact recalled 24 of 24 in both runs and was the cheapest arm at 0.98 and 0.96 USD; verbatim also recalled 24 of 24 at 1.09 and 1.10 USD.
- The implicit cache read 58 to 64 percent of the input for compact and verbatim, but 0 to 18 percent for every extract arm, against 79 to 84 percent for the same extract arms on DeepSeek-V4-Flash (decisive-1500).
- extract with statement-triggered extraction recalled 24 of 24 in both runs at 1.24 and 1.18 USD; apu-urithi's routing recalled 22 and 24; the cascade router recalled 21 and 18.

**Limits.**

- Two runs per arm; no naive arm. The implicit cache is best effort: on 2026-10-27 the same model gave 0 hits (docs/measured/probes/).
- One generated English scenario; recall is a substring check of 24 facts, answer quality is not judged.
